What you can listen to with Noesis

Noesis reads documents aloud in a natural voice made on your own device. It is free to use, works offline, and nothing you add is uploaded. One page for each thing it does:

  • Listen to a PDF
  • Noesis next to other reader apps
  • PDF to audio: Turn a PDF into audio with Noesis: a natural voice made on your own phone or computer reads it to you, and you can save the audio as a file.
  • Text to speech: Paste any text into Noesis and hear it in a natural voice made on your own device.
  • EPUB to audiobook: Open an EPUB book in Noesis and listen to it as an audiobook: a natural voice made on your own device, chapters kept, your place remembered.
  • Listen to research papers: Give Noesis a paper as a PDF or an arXiv link and hear it read as prose: no page numbers, running headers or reference lists.
  • Web article to audio: Paste the link of an article into Noesis and hear it in a natural voice made on your own device.
  • Word document to audio: Open a Word document (.docx) in Noesis and hear it read in a natural voice made on your own device.
  • Hindi text to speech: Hear documents in Hindi with Noesis: a natural Hindi voice made on your own device, and free translation of English documents into Hindi.
  • Telugu text to speech: Hear documents in Telugu with Noesis: natural Telugu voices made on your own device, and free translation of English documents into Telugu.
