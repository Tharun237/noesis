Noesis

Text to speech that runs on your own device

Paste text into Noesis and a natural voice reads it to you. The voice is made by your own phone or computer: the text is not sent to a server, it works with no connection, and it is free to use.

Open Noesis

How to hear a text

  1. Open Noesis and choose Add, then Text.
  2. Paste the text. Markdown headings are understood, so a long text keeps its chapters.
  3. Press Play. The first time, the voice downloads once (about 60 MB). After that it works with no connection.

More than a read-aloud button

  • The word being said is lit as it is said, so you can read along.
  • Focus shows one word at a time, large, for reading without moving your eyes.
  • It remembers your place, and keeps every text you add in a library.
  • Speed, sleep timer and bookmarks work as in any player.

Voices and languages

There are natural voices for English, Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Vietnamese. Each voice downloads once and then works offline. If you would rather not wait for a download, the voice built into your device can read instead.

Questions

Is there a limit on how much text?

No. A whole book can be pasted. Your device makes the audio as you listen.

Does it need the internet?

Only to open the app the first time and to download a voice once. After that it works with no connection.

Where does my text go?

Nowhere. It is kept in your browser, on your device.
