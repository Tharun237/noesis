Noesis

Listen to research papers, without the page furniture

Give Noesis a paper, as a PDF or as an arXiv link, and it is read to you as prose. Page numbers, running headers and the reference list are left out, and a table is described instead of recited. It is free to use and runs on your own device.

Open Noesis

How to listen to a paper

  1. Open Noesis and add the PDF, or choose Link and paste the paper's address. An arXiv link fetches the paper itself, not the abstract page.
  2. Press Play. The first time, the voice downloads once (about 60 MB). After that it works with no connection.

Made for papers

  • Only what matters is read. Headers, footers, page numbers and the reference list are skipped.
  • Figures, tables and equations are marked, and you can choose to skip them while listening.
  • Follow on the original pages, with the sentence being read marked on the page.
  • The works a passage cites can be opened from the passage.
  • Hard words in the paper are listed, most used first.

With your own AI key

Noesis has no AI subscription of its own. If you add a key from your own Google or NVIDIA account, it can also summarise a paper, answer questions about it, take an equation apart step by step and describe a figure. Without a key, everything above still works.

Questions

Does it read the equations?

Equations are marked as equations. With your own AI key an equation can be explained step by step; without one you can have equations skipped.

Is the paper uploaded?

A PDF you add is read by your own browser. For a link, the page is fetched for you and handed to your device; it is not kept.

Can I listen to it in my own language?

Yes. It can be translated on your device and read in English, Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Vietnamese.
