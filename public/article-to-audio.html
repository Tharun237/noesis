Noesis

Listen to any web article by its link

Paste the link of an article into Noesis and it is read to you in a natural voice. It joins your library, so a reading list becomes a listening list. It is free to use.

Open Noesis

How to listen to an article

  1. Open Noesis, choose Add, then Link.
  2. Paste the address of the article.
  3. Press Play. The first time, the voice downloads once (about 60 MB). After that it works with no connection.

What happens to the page

  • The article is kept; the menus and the rest of the page around it are left out.
  • It is saved in your library with its place, so you can stop and come back.
  • Queue several: with autoplay turned on in Settings, the next one starts when one ends.

What to know first

  • A browser does not let one site read another, so the page is fetched for you by Noesis and handed to your device. It is not kept.
  • A page behind a login or a paywall cannot be fetched. Paste its text instead.

Questions

Does it work on any site?

On pages anyone can open. A page that needs a login cannot be fetched; its text can be pasted in instead.

Can I listen later with no connection?

Yes. Once an article is in your library and its audio is made, it plays offline.

Is there a browser extension?

No. Noesis is a web app you can add to your home screen; links are pasted into it.
