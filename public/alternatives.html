Noesis

A free alternative to Speechify, ElevenReader, NaturalReader and Paper2Audio

If you came here looking for another app that reads documents aloud, this page tells you plainly what Noesis is, so you can decide in a minute whether it suits you. It is free to use, and you can try it without making an account.

Five questions worth asking of any reader app

1. Does my document leave my device?

In Noesis, no. The file is read on your device and the voice is made on your device. Nothing is uploaded to make the audio.

2. Does it work with no connection?

Yes. A voice downloads once (about 60 MB). After that you can add documents and listen on a plane.

3. What does it cost, and is there a limit?

Noesis is free to use. It sets no limit on pages, minutes or documents, because your own device does the work.

4. Do I need an account?

No. If you want your library on a second device, you can sign in with Google, and it is then kept in a private folder of your own Google Drive. Noesis keeps no copy.

5. Does it understand what a paper or a book is?

It leaves out page numbers, running headers and reference lists, says what a table holds, starts a book at the book and not at its copyright page, and lets you follow in clean text, on the original pages, or one word at a time.

What else is in it

  • PDF, EPUB, Word documents, web pages by their link, and pasted text.
  • A choice of natural voices, a speed control, bookmarks and a sleep timer.
  • Spoken commands while you listen, in browsers that support them.
  • The audio saved as a file, and the text as a transcript.
  • Listening in another language, free: your device translates a document between English and Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Arabic.
  • With a key from your own Google or NVIDIA account: summaries, questions about the document, explanations, and more natural translations.

Where another app may suit you better

  • If you want a celebrity or a cloned voice. Noesis has a fixed set of natural voices that run on your device.
  • If your phone is old. Your device makes the audio. It plays as it goes, but a long book takes longer to finish making on a slow phone.
  • If you want an app from an app store. Noesis opens in your browser and can be added to your home screen from there.
  • If you want AI features without setting anything up. In Noesis they use a key from your own account, which takes a few minutes the first time.
