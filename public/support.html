What this payment is

  • It is voluntary. Nothing in the app is unlocked by paying, and nothing is held back if you do not.
  • It is a payment to the maker, not a gift to a charity. There is no tax receipt for giving.
  • It is taken on Razorpay's payment page, in the name of Aetheral Catch Seafoods, the maker's company. That is the name you will see on the payment page and on your statement.
  • Noesis never sees your card or bank details. They are entered on Razorpay's page, not here, and nothing about your library goes with them.
  • What it pays for: the address you are on, and the time to keep building.
