Hindi text to speech: listen to PDFs and books in Hindi

Noesis reads documents aloud in Hindi with a natural voice made on your own device. A Hindi document is read as it is; an English one can be translated into Hindi on your device first. It is free to use.

How to listen in Hindi

  1. Open Noesis and add a PDF, a book, a link or pasted text.
  2. If the document is in English, open its menu, choose Listen in another language, then Hindi. The translator downloads once (20 to 55 MB) and then works offline.
  3. Press Play. The Hindi voice downloads once, then works with no connection.

What to know first

  • Translation is done by your own device. It is plain and accurate rather than literary; with your own AI key it reads more naturally.
  • A long book can be translated a chapter at a time, and you are told how long it will take before you start.
  • A Hindi document can also be heard in English, or in another of the languages Noesis reads.

Questions

Is the Hindi voice free?

Yes. It runs on your device, so nothing is charged for using it.

Can it read a Hindi PDF?

Yes, if the PDF holds real text. A scanned page is read from the text stored in the scan.

Does translation need the internet?

Only to download the translator once. After that it works offline.
