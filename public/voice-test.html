Voice test

This measures, on THIS device, how fast two voices can be made: the one Noesis uses today (Piper, Kristin) and Kitten TTS. Each says the same three sentences. A voice has to be made faster than it is listened to: under 1x it cannot keep up with a book.

The voices are downloaded once and kept on this device for the test (Kitten nano 24 MB, Kitten mini 78 MB, Piper 63 MB unless Noesis already has it). Use Wi-Fi.
