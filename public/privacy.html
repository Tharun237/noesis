Noesis

Privacy

Last changed 12 October 2026

Noesis runs on your own device. Your documents, the audio made from them and your settings are kept in your browser, on that device. Noesis has no advertising and no analytics, and it does not sell or share information about you.

If you do not sign in

Nothing about you or your library is stored by Noesis. Three things do go over the network:

  • A web link you add. Browsers do not let one site read another, so Noesis's server fetches the page for you and hands it to your device. The page is not kept on the server.
  • A voice, the first time you use it. Its files are downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which sees that request the way any website sees a visitor. After that the voice works without a connection.
  • The app itself. Your browser fetches Noesis and its updates from Cloudflare, which hosts it and, like any host, sees the address the request came from.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It exists for one purpose: to keep the same library on your phone and your computer.

Your library is then kept in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that Google gives to each app. You do not see it among your files, no other app can open it, and Noesis can open nothing else in your Drive. It uses your own Drive storage. What is kept there:

  • the text of your documents and their details (title, author, kind, length);
  • your place in each document;
  • your bookmarks and notes;
  • your collections.

Noesis keeps no copy of any of it. It has no database of accounts or libraries. Audio is never uploaded, and neither are your original files: each device makes its own audio from the text.

From Google, Noesis receives your name, email address, profile picture and Google account number, used only to show which account is signed in, and a renewal token, which lets the app reach that one folder without asking you to sign in every hour. These are not stored on Noesis's server either: they are kept in a cookie in your own browser, locked with a key only the server has. To renew its hourly pass for your Drive, your browser sends that cookie to Noesis's server, which asks Google and hands the pass back; nothing is written down.

You can sign out at any time in Settings. To remove what is kept, and Noesis's access with it, open Google Drive's settings on the web, choose Manage apps, and under Noesis choose "Delete hidden app data" and then "Disconnect from Drive". Nothing then remains anywhere but on your own devices.

If you turn on AI features

They are off until you add a key of your own, and the key is kept on your device.

  • Google Gemini: the text you ask about goes straight from your device to Google, under your own account with them.
  • NVIDIA: NVIDIA does not accept requests from a browser, so your key and the text pass through Noesis's server on their way. Neither is stored there.
  • Another service you name: the text goes to the address you gave.

What those services do with what they receive is set by your agreement with them, not by Noesis.

Cookies

One, and only if you sign in: the locked cookie described above, which keeps you signed in. There are no tracking cookies.
