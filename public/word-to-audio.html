Listen to a Word document

Open a Word document in Noesis and hear it read to you. The voice is made on your own device, so the document stays with you. It is free to use.

Open Noesis

How to listen to a Word document

  1. Open Noesis and add the .docx file.
  2. Press Play. The first time, the voice downloads once (about 60 MB). After that it works with no connection.

Useful for your own writing

  • Hearing a draft shows what the eye skips: a missing word, a sentence that runs too long.
  • The word being said is lit, so you can find the place in your document.
  • Headings are kept, so a long report has its sections.

What to know first

  • The file must be .docx. An older .doc file needs saving as .docx first.
  • Nothing is changed in your document; Noesis only reads it.

Questions

Is my document private?

Yes. It is read by your own browser and kept on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the app has been opened once and a voice has been downloaded.

What about Google Docs?

Download the document as .docx or PDF, or paste its text.
