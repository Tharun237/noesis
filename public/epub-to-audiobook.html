Noesis

Turn an EPUB into an audiobook, free

Open an EPUB in Noesis and listen to it like an audiobook. The voice is made on your own device, the chapters are kept, and your place is remembered. It is free to use and works offline.

Open Noesis

How to listen to an EPUB

  1. Open Noesis and add the EPUB file.
  2. Press Play. The first time, the voice downloads once (about 60 MB). After that it works with no connection.
  3. Use Contents to move between chapters.

What you get

  • The book starts at the book, not at the copyright page.
  • A sleep timer that can stop at the end of the chapter.
  • Your place, on every device, if you sign in: the library is kept in your own Google Drive.
  • A line that follows the mood of the book, so you can see the heavy chapter coming.
  • The audio as a file, if you want to take it to another player.

What to know first

  • The book must be an EPUB without copy protection. Books bought inside a store app are usually locked to that app and cannot be opened by anything else.
  • A long book takes your device a while to turn into audio. You can start within seconds; the rest is made ahead of you.

Questions

Does it work with Kindle books?

Only with books that are not copy-protected. Most books bought in a store app are locked to it.

Can I listen in another language?

Yes. A book can be translated on your device and read in English, Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Vietnamese. You can translate one chapter or the whole book.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Google is optional and only keeps the same library on your phone and your computer.
