Why open

Noesis makes promises about your documents. They stay on your device. The voice that reads them is made there. Signing in keeps your library in your own Google Drive, not on a server of ours. Your AI key stays with you, and nothing is sent to an AI until you turn it on.

A promise you can check is worth more than one you have to believe. Now anyone can read exactly what Noesis does with a document, from the moment it is added to the moment it is heard.

What the licence means for you

  • To listen, nothing changes. Noesis is free to use.
  • To read and learn from it, it is all there.
  • To run your own copy, for yourself or for others, you may. The code says how.
  • To change it, you may. If you let other people use your changed copy, over the web too, the licence asks you to give them its source under the same terms. That keeps every copy as open as this one.

This is a plain summary. The licence itself is the GNU AGPL-3.0.

What is not ours to give

Noesis stands on work other people shared freely: the voices and the recordings they were trained on, the translation models, the fonts, the libraries. Each keeps its own licence, and the app lists every one, with a link, in Settings → About → Credits and licences.

How it is made

  • Your device does the work. The voice, the translation and the reading run in your browser. It keeps working without a connection.
  • One of everything. Each screen has one purpose, each control one place. If a control needs a sentence to explain it, the design is wrong, not the label.
  • Motion means something. Things move to show where they came from and where they went, and only in ways a phone can draw smoothly.
  • Made for people who get interrupted. Phones close apps halfway through. What you did survives it.
  • Tested as a customer. An impatient random customer taps through the whole app, and transitions are filmed frame by frame, before a change is called done.

These rules are written down with the code, in .claude/skills, and a change is judged by them.

Take part

Found something wrong? Tell us in an issue. Want to build something? Read how to contribute first: it says what a change must keep true, and the terms a contribution is given under.

Noesis is made by one person. If it is worth something to you, you can support it. Nothing in the app changes either way.
