Noesis

Listen to a PDF, offline and without uploading it

Open a PDF in Noesis and it is read to you in a natural voice. The voice runs on your own phone or computer, so the file never leaves your device and it keeps working with no connection. It is free to use.

Open Noesis

How it works

  1. Open Noesis in your browser. There is nothing to install and no account to make.
  2. Add your PDF. It is read on your device, and the text appears as it is read.
  3. Press Play. The first time, the voice downloads once (about 60 MB). After that it works offline.

What it does with a PDF

  • Reads what matters. Page numbers, running headers and reference lists are left out, so a paper sounds like prose and not like a photocopy.
  • Says what a table holds instead of reading its cells in a row.
  • Shows you where it is. Follow along in clean text, on the original pages with the sentence marked, or one word at a time.
  • Starts at the book, not at the copyright page.
  • Remembers your place, lets you bookmark a sentence, change the speed, and set a sleep timer.
  • Saves the audio as a file if you want to take it elsewhere.

Not only PDFs

The same goes for EPUB books, Word documents, web pages by their link, and text you paste in.

In your own language

A document in English can be heard in Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Arabic, and a document in any of those can be heard in English or in another of them. Your device does the translating, free: the first time you choose a language, its translator downloads once (20 to 55 MB) and then works offline.

If you add your own AI key

Noesis has no AI subscription of its own. If you give it a key from your own account with Google or NVIDIA, it can also summarise a document, answer questions about it, explain a passage, and describe figures and equations. It also makes translations more natural. Without a key, everything above this paragraph still works.

What to know before you start

  • The audio is made by your own device. It starts playing once enough is made, within seconds on a recent device, and the rest is made ahead of you as you listen. On an older phone that takes longer.
  • A scanned PDF is read from the text stored in the scan. If that text is poor, the reading will be too, unless you turn on AI narration with your own key.
  • Noesis runs in a current browser. You can add it to your home screen, and it then opens like an app.
