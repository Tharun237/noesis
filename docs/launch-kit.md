# Noesis: words for the people telling others about it

For the marketing team. Everything here is true of the app as it is on 13 October 2026; if the app changes,
change this. Say nothing about other products. Never say "forever".

## In one line
Noesis reads your PDFs, books and articles aloud in a natural voice made on your own phone. Free to use, works offline.

## In fifty words
Add a PDF, an EPUB, a Word file, a link or pasted text, and Noesis reads it to you. The voice is made by your own
device, so nothing is uploaded and it works with no connection. Follow in text, on the original pages, or one
word at a time. Hear it in nine languages. No app store: it opens in the browser.

## Five facts worth repeating
1. Free to use, no account, no advertising.
2. The file never leaves your device.
3. Works offline after the first voice download (about 60 MB, once).
4. English documents can be heard in Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Vietnamese,
   translated on the device at no cost.
5. Installs from the browser: no Play Store, no App Store.

weeks to appear. Compare week on week, not day on day.
