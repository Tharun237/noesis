## Platform

web

## Users

Confirmed by Tharun (1 November 2026), all four:

- **Students and researchers** getting through papers, textbooks and long PDFs, often away from a desk or tired of
  reading on a screen.
- **Book lovers who listen**: people who want any book read to them, free, in a natural voice.
- **People who cannot read easily**: low vision, dyslexia, tired eyes, reading in a second language.
- **Everyone, India first**: a general app, with Indian languages and ordinary Indian phones as the first focus.

## Product Purpose

Noesis turns documents and books into listening: a natural voice, made on the customer's own device, reads them
aloud, with the text, the original page or one word at a time to follow along. Success is someone finishing a paper
or a book they would not otherwise have finished.

## Positioning

Everything heavy runs on the customer's own device, and what is kept is kept in the customer's own Google Drive:
there is no account database, no advertising, and nothing whose cost grows with customers. AI and online voices use
the customer's own key. The code is public (GNU AGPL-3.0), so every promise can be checked.

## Operating Context

Used on phones first (often modest Android phones, often while moving), and on laptops. Phones close the app halfway
through; every write must survive that. A customer may use several devices with one account and expects the same
library, place and settings on each.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Adds PDF, EPUB, Word, web links, Markdown and text; reads in 9 languages with 19 on-device voices; translates on
  the device; Ask the document with the customer's own AI key; Text, Pages and Focus views; sync through the
  customer's Drive.
- **Books tab (2026-11-01):** explores free books. Only books free to use in India and almost every country:
  every named person (author, translator, editor, illustrator) died more than 70 years ago. Source: Project
  Gutenberg's published catalogue, copied to Noesis's own site; the book itself downloads from Gutenberg when the
  customer chooses it. 32,723 books on the first build.
- **Browsing is not adding.** Tapping a book opens its page; the customer chooses to Listen (try it) or Add to
  library. A book being tried stays under "Listening to" in Books on that device, with its place, until it is added
  or removed. It is not in the library, not synced, until added.
- No shadow libraries or copied books, ever (Anna's Archive was asked about and refused: illegal).
- Deploys only when Tharun says "Ship it".

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Noesis. Pure black page, white type, lime only for highlights (the word being spoken, a "New" mark).
- Plain words a customer already uses; one of everything on a screen (`.claude/skills/design`).
- Motion by transform and opacity only, meaning something (`.claude/skills/motion`).
- "Free to use" may be said; never "forever"; no price promises (`.claude/skills/goods-and-services`).

## Evidence on Hand

- The live app at www.noesisaudio.com and its public code.
- No testimonials, user counts or reviews exist: none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The customer's device does the work; nothing is uploaded to make audio.
2. Every claim on a screen is true of the app as it is today.
3. The customer decides: nothing is added, sent or paid for that they did not choose.
4. Made for interrupted people on modest phones: fast, and nothing lost.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Many users cannot read easily: large readable type, 44-pixel targets, screen-reader labels, Focus view needs no
sound, reduced motion respected (fades instead of movement).
